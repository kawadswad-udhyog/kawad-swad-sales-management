## 1. Core Architectural Invariants (Sections 1–6)

1. **Google Sheets Sole Database (`POL-001`)**: Google Sheets is the sole authoritative business database. MongoDB, PostgreSQL, MySQL, and SQLite are strictly prohibited in application architecture and runtime code.
2. **Apps Script Single Governed Write Owner (`POL-002`)**: FastAPI (`backend/app/`) performs read/query operations, deterministic calculation previews, validation, RBAC, and HMAC-signed delegation only. Direct FastAPI Google Sheets mutations (`append_record`, `update_record`, `delete_record`, `generate_id`, `get_next_id`, `allocate_bill_number`, `make_id`) are prohibited across `backend/app/`. All mutations execute exclusively via `apps_script_bridge.execute_governed_write` / `delegate_governed_write`.
3. **255-Tab Cleaned Workbook & 46 Deleted Legacy Scratch Tabs (`POL-003`)**: The ERP operates across the cleaned 255-tab workbook organized into 15 functional database groups (`00`–`14`, codified in `backend/app/schema_registry.py::DATABASE_GROUPS_15`). The 46 deleted legacy scratch tab prefixes (`KS_P1_`, `KS_P2_`, `KS_P3_`, `DBV5_`, `DB_`, excluding the authorized dictionary `DBV5_FIELD_DICTIONARY_3074`) are blocked fail-closed (`403 SCRATCH_TAB_FORBIDDEN`) and must never be recreated.
4. **32 Locked Official Business ID Prefixes (`POL-004`)**: Sequential IDs are allocated atomically by Google Apps Script under `LOCKED_32_ID_PREFIXES` (`PRD`, `SKU`, `RM`, `PKG`, `CUST`, `SUP`, `USR`, `EMP`, `LOT`, `MOV`, `PO`, `PB`, `MCON`, `POUT`, `PLOSS`, `PWE`, `IPQC`, `FGQC`, `PKB`, `PKOUT`, `SO`, `SOI`, `INV`, `INVI`, `RCPT`, `RCPTA`, `JV`, `JVL`, `SAL`, `EADV`, `AUD`, `EXC`). Unapproved scratch prefixes (`DSP`/`DISP`, `CHN`, `CMP`, `AST`, `RPT`) are prohibited.
5. **No Row-1 Header Fabrication (`POL-005`)**: Tabs whose physical Row-1 spreadsheet headers have not been supplied from the authoritative master `.xlsx` workbook are registered in `EXTENDED_V2_TAB_CONTRACTS` / `PENDING_OWNER_MAPPING_TABS` with `headers: None` and `auto_create: False`. Writes targeting unmapped tabs fail closed with `503 BLOCKED_PENDING_OWNER_SETUP`.
6. **Exception Tab Reconciliation (`POL-006`)**: Prefix `EXC` is reconciled to canonical tab name `ExceptionRegister` matching `AuditAndExceptions.gs` in the verified 255-tab workbook (`ExceptionLogs` deprecated via `DEPRECATED_TAB_ALIASES`), while physical Row-1 column headers remain `PENDING_OWNER_MAPPING` until supplied by Owner.
